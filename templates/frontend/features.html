Features

Everything KIPA does, designed for speed, security, and simplicity.

Privacy First

Raw face template images are never stored. Only 128-dimension vector calculations are mapped, then discarded after matching.

Sub-Second Matching

Search indexes process thousands of files and return exact faces in milliseconds — no waiting, no scrolling.

Delegated Assistants

Create accounts for event assistants with granular permissions to upload, delete, or manage photos only.

Liveness Protection

Anti-spoof challenges (open your mouth, blink) block screens, prints, and masks from stealing gallery privacy.

Bulk Upload & Auto Indexing

Drag and drop hundreds of high-res photos. KIPA automatically detects faces and builds a searchable index.

Controlled Downloads

Set per-event download limits so every guest can save their photos without overusing the system.

Adjustable Match Sensitivity

Strict or relaxed matching thresholds let you tune how exact a face match must be for each event.

Event Lifecycle Management

Publish, unpublish, and auto-deactivate events by date, keeping galleries fresh and controlled.
