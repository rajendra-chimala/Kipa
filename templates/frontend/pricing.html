Pricing FAQ

Everything you need to know about KIPA pricing.

Do guests need to pay to find their photos?
Never. Photo retrieval for guests is always free. Photographers and studios pay a simple subscription fee.
Can I change or cancel my plan anytime?
Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time from your dashboard. No lock-in contracts.
What counts as a “photo”?
Each uploaded image counts as one photo. Photos already delivered to guests never count against your quota again.
