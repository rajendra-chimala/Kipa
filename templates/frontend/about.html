About KIPA

KIPA (किपा) means “memory” — built to reunite people with their favourite moments.

Our Mission

Every event photo should reach the right person, in seconds, without compromising privacy.

After every wedding, conference, concert or school function, photographers are left delivering thousands of photos to hundreds of people. Attendees are left scrolling through endless galleries hoping to spot themselves. KIPA solves both problems at once: photographers upload once, and every guest retrieves their own photos by scanning their face — in milliseconds, with the strongest privacy protections.

Built for Photographers, Trusted by Guests

KIPA started from a simple frustration: a wedding photographer friend spent weeks sending photo links one by one. We combined AI face recognition with strict privacy engineering so that beautiful memories reach the right people faster than ever — while raw face data is never stored or sold.

What We Stand For

The principles behind every line of code and every photo delivered.

Privacy First

Raw face images from scans are never stored. Only transient 128-dimension vector hashes are used for matching.

Radical Speed

Sub-second matching means guests never wait — even across galleries with thousands of high-resolution photos.

Empowering Photographers

From solo shooters to studios with assistant teams, KIPA gives every photographer an effortless delivery workflow.

Trust Through Transparency

Liveness checks, download limits and clear controls keep galleries safe and honest for hosts and guests alike.
