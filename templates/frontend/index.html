Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about KIPA.

Is my biometric face data stored permanently?
No. KIPA does not store any face templates or raw photos of guest face scans in the database. Face details are converted into transient 128-point vector numbers during scanning, which are deleted as soon as the matches are calculated and returned.
What is anti-spoof liveness protection?
To protect attendee privacy, KIPA runs a multi-challenge liveness check (like opening your mouth or blinking). This guarantees that a live person is standing before the camera, preventing anyone from holding up printouts or screen displays of your face to steal your photos.
How can I upload photos as a photographer?
Photographers can log in via the Photographer Portal button. From there, you can create events, configure download limits, and drag-and-drop photos in bulk for AI processing.
Can I restrict the number of downloads per guest?
Yes. Each event can be configured with a strict download limit count. Once a guest downloads their matches up to this threshold within their browser session, the server blocks further downloads to prevent system overuse.
