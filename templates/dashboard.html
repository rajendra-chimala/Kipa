Quick Guide

Welcome to your FaceSnap Photographer Dashboard. From here you can:

  • Create photography events and set their status to published or unpublished.
  • Add deactivation dates so events automatically expire.
  • Configure a **Download Limit** per photo. Photos are tracked by visitor sessions, and will hide automatically once downloaded.
  • Add Assistant sub-users to assist with uploads, deletions, and event deactivation.
